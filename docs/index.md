**RAcontact helps Residential Assistants (RAs) at NUS hostels find the contacts they need on duty.**
Keep residents, fellow RAs, hall staff, vendors and emergency services in one place, instead of searching through chats and documents.

Use short typed commands to search by name, phone, room or tag. Mark frequently used contacts as favourites,
record a resident's emergency contact, and archive old contacts while keeping their details for later.
The graphical contact list lets you read the results at a glance.

* Start using RAcontact with the [_Quick Start_ section of the **User Guide**](UserGuide.html#quick-start).
* Learn how RAcontact is built in the [**Developer Guide**](DeveloperGuide.html).
